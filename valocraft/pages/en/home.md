---

**Valocraft** is a from-scratch Valorant recreation for Minecraft servers. Two teams of up to five play spike rounds with a Valorant-style economy, hitscan weapons, agent abilities and ultimates. There are also quick modes, deathmatch and Escalation.

> ⚠️ Valocraft 2.x is a complete rewrite. Nothing from 1.x remains, and 1.x configs and maps are not compatible.

## 🚀 Quick start

1. [Install the plugin](page:installation) on a Paper 1.21.4+ server.
2. Set the lobby with `/vc setlobby`.
3. [Build a map](page:map-setup) or [import one](page:importing-maps).
4. Join from the lobby ([Lobby and Matchmaking](page:lobby)) and play.

## ✨ Highlights

- 🎭 **29 agents** (Duelists, Initiators, Controllers, Sentinels), with their **full skin** in match
- 🎮 **8 modes**: Unrated, Competitive, Swiftplay, Replication, Spike Rush, Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, Escalation
- ⚡ **Faithful abilities**: white flashes, throw trajectories, Neon and Viper gauges, destructible utility, first-person drone piloting
- 🥇 **Ranked** with ranks and RR, a 30-level **battle pass**, daily missions
- 🎨 **Cosmetics**: weapon skins, knives, cards (banners), titles, kill banners and kill sounds, sprays
- 🏆 **Hologram leaderboards** in the lobby
- 🌍 **On-demand map loading**, with a loading screen showing every player's card and title
- 🔫 **Hitscan weapons** with continuous fire, recoil, penetration, lag compensation and 3D models
- 🗺️ **In-game map editor** and import of Ommo's Valorant maps
- 📦 **Self-hosted resource pack** served on the Minecraft port (Pterodactyl-friendly)
