**Valocraft** is a from-scratch recreation of Valorant for Minecraft servers. Two teams of up to five play spike rounds with a Valorant-style economy, hitscan weapons, agent abilities and ultimates.

> ⚠️ Valocraft 2.x is a complete rewrite. Nothing from the 1.x versions exists anymore, and 1.x configs and maps are not compatible.

## 🚀 Quick start

1. [Install the plugin](page:installation) on a Paper 1.21.4+ server.
2. Set the lobby with `/vc setlobby`.
3. [Create a map](page:map-setup) or [import one](page:importing-maps).
4. Join from the lobby ([Lobby and Matchmaking](page:lobby)) and play.

## ✨ Highlights

- 🎭 **15 agents** across Duelists, Initiators, Controllers and Sentinels
- ⚡ **Ultimate system** fed by kills, deaths, spike actions and ultimate orbs
- 🧭 **Ziplines** you can ride and shoot from
- 🏟️ **Lobby with matchmaking**, host-your-own games, stats and leaderboards
- 🔫 **Hitscan weapons** with spread, recoil, penetration and 3D models
- 🗺️ **In-game map editor** and importer for Ommo's Valorant maps
- 📦 **Self-hosted resource pack** served on the Minecraft port (Pterodactyl compatible)
