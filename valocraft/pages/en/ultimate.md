# Ultimate System


Every agent has an ultimate on key **8** (X). It costs a number of **ultimate points** (6 to 9 depending on the agent, listed on the [Agents](page:agents) page).

## Earning points

You gain ultimate points from:

- **Kills**
- **Deaths**
- **Spike plant**
- **Spike defuse**
- **Ultimate orbs** (see below)

Points are **kept at halftime**.

## Feedback

| Moment | What happens |
|---|---|
| Progress | Always shown on the action bar (`X ●●●○○○`). Pressing **8** shows your ultimate and its point count. |
| **Ultimate ready** | Title on screen, a sound, and a message to your whole team. |
| **Ultimate cast** | Everyone hears the agent's voice line (HrdaValorant pack) and the chat announces it. |

## Ultimates that change key 8

Some ultimates turn key 8 into a weapon while active:

- Blade Storm
- Hunter's Fury
- Showstopper
- Overdrive

**Blade Storm**, **Overdrive** and **Empress** are extended or refilled by **eliminations**.

## Instant ultimates

These activate the moment you press the key: **Run it Back**, **Empress**, **Lockdown**, **Viper's Pit** and **Seekers**.

## Ultimate orbs

Orbs are a map feature placed by admins.

- **Place**: `/vc map addorb <map>` at the position you stand on.
- **Remove all**: `/vc map clearorbs <map>`.
- **Respawn**: orbs reappear **every round**.
- **Collect**: **hold right click for 1.5 s** next to an orb to gain **+1 ultimate point**.

See [Map Setup](page:map-setup) for the full map workflow.
